# 🩺 LifeLink — Beta Release

Thank you for helping test **LifeLink**! This document explains what you're installing, what to expect, and how to share feedback.

---

## What is LifeLink?

LifeLink is an emergency response app that connects **civilians** with nearby **emergency agencies** — Police, Hospitals, Fire Stations, and Pharmacies — for faster help during a crisis.

- **Civilians** can send an SOS, view nearby emergency services on a map, check activity/analytics, and access quick links like Blood Bank and Find Emergency.
- **Agency accounts** (Police / Hospital / Fire Station / Pharmacy) get their own dashboard to view and respond to incoming reports/requests from civilians in their area.

---

## What is this "Beta"?

This is a **pre-release build**, shared directly as an **APK file** (Android only) — not through the Google Play Store. It's an early version meant for a small group of testers to try the app before a public release.

Because it's a beta:
- You may encounter bugs, crashes, or unfinished screens (some sections are marked "Coming soon").
- Features may change, be removed, or behave differently in the final release.
- Data created during testing (accounts, reports, etc.) may be reset before the official launch.

---

## How to Install (Android APK)

1. Download the `.apk` file from the link shared with you.
2. On your Android phone, open the downloaded file.
3. If prompted, allow **"Install from unknown sources"** for the app you used to download it (Files, Chrome, WhatsApp, etc.) — this is required since the app isn't from the Play Store.
4. Tap **Install**, then open LifeLink.

> ⚠️ This build is Android-only for now. There is no iOS beta at this stage.

## Signing Up

- Civilian accounts: register normally with your email (OTP verification) inside the app.
- Agency accounts (Police/Hospital/Fire/Pharmacy): these are typically created/approved separately — if you're testing an agency role, you'll be given login credentials directly.

## What We'd Love Feedback On

- 🐞 **Bugs** — anything that crashes, freezes, or behaves unexpectedly
- 🎨 **UI/UX** — anything confusing, hard to find, or awkward to use
- ⚡ **Performance** — slow screens, laggy maps, delayed notifications
- 📍 **Location/Map accuracy** — is nearby data showing correctly for your area
- 🔔 **Notifications** — are SOS/agency alerts arriving as expected

Please include:
- What you were doing when the issue happened
- Your device model & Android version
- A screenshot or screen recording, if possible

---

## Known Limitations (Beta)

- Some screens (e.g. Blood Bank, Find Emergency) are placeholders and not fully functional yet.
- Push notifications may be delayed or occasionally not arrive.
- This build points to a test backend — data may be cleared without notice.

---

## Privacy Note

This is a testing build. Please avoid entering real sensitive medical or personal information you wouldn't want reset or exposed to the dev team during testing.
